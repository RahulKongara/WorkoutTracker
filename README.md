# WorkoutTracker

## Interactive Workout Tracker (Still under development)

> [!NOTE] Check Main branch for the status of the tracker.

## Features

### User Authentication (❗Under Dev)

- To keep track of your workout data along with your personal metrics.
- This further helps keep track of your consistency in the form of [streak].

---

### Workouts Page

- Get access to the entire workout gallery.
- This Page provides step-by-step instruction guide to perform an exercise.

---

### Split Page (❗Under Dev)

- Choose from popular splits or pre-made workout splits.
- Create your own workout split as per your needs.

---

### [Streak] (❗Under Dev)

- Keep track of your consistency with the built-in dynamic streak mechanism.
- All you need to do is login your workout everyday.
